Booking & Rates04

You can reserve directly through our website — choose your dates, select a pavilion, and submit an enquiry. Our team will confirm availability and rates within the day.

For special arrangements — weddings, corporate retreats or full-estate buyouts — please reach us at hello@singgah-sana.com and we'll respond with a tailored proposal.

Rates start from RM 750 per night for Rumah Bunga, and up to RM 1,200 per night for Rumah Ibu. All rates include a catered breakfast each morning, daily housekeeping, and full access to the pool, gardens and pond.

Final pricing is confirmed by our team based on your dates and party size, and includes applicable taxes and services.

A 50% deposit confirms your reservation. The balance is due 14 days before arrival. We accept credit and debit cards, FPX online banking, and bank transfer.

A confirmation email with your booking details and receipt will be sent once payment is received.

Free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival. Within 14 days of arrival, the deposit is non-refundable. Cancellation terms are confirmed at the time of booking and included in your reservation.

We recommend travel insurance for any unplanned changes.

The Stay04

Check-in from 3:00 PM. Check-out by 12:00 PM. Early check-in or late check-out may be arranged on request, subject to availability — just let us know in advance.

Two nights minimum on weekends. Three nights on public holidays and peak seasons. Longer minimums may apply over festive periods — our team will confirm at the time of booking.

Every pavilion includes organic cotton bedding and robes, a mosquito net, an in-room safe, filtered drinking water, and premium toiletries. All rooms have air conditioning and a private verandah or deck.

Full pavilion details — including amenities, inclusions and rates — are on the Stay page.

Yes. Full-estate buyouts are available and often preferred for weddings, corporate retreats, multi-family gatherings and workshops. When the estate is reserved, no other guests are on the property.

See Moments for capacity details, or write to us for a tailored proposal.

Dining03

Yes — breakfast is included with every stay. It's served at the open-air dining pavilion, with local fruit, eggs from the valley, and coffee or tea. We can adjust for dietary preferences with advance notice.

Yes. Our private chef can prepare lunch and dinner throughout your stay — a mix of local and international dishes, planned with you ahead of arrival. Please allow 24 hours' notice for special menus.

Yes. We can arrange vegetarian, vegan, halal, and allergy-aware menus — just let us know your requirements when you book, or at least 24 hours before your meal. Our chef will confirm details with you directly.

Activities03

On the estate: sunrise yoga on the deck, fishing at the pond (fresh tilapia, catch and release), swimming, reading on the verandah, walking the four acres of grounds, and long, unhurried meals. In-villa massage can be arranged on request.

See Things to Do for the full list.

Waterfalls and jungle treks, river bathing and picnics, organic farm visits, natural hot springs, and quiet road cycling through the kampung hills — all within a short drive of the estate.

Our team is happy to arrange guides, drivers, or a suggested itinerary.

Yes. In-villa massage — traditional Malay and deep-tissue treatments — can be arranged in your pavilion. We also work with visiting wellness practitioners for yoga, breathwork and multi-day retreats.

Children & Pets02

Families are very welcome. Please let us know the ages of your children and any specific needs ahead of your stay — we can prepare cots, high chairs, or simple bedding arrangements on request.

We're currently not able to accommodate pets, to protect the estate's gardens, the pond, and the surrounding wildlife. Thank you for understanding.

Getting Here03

Singgah Sana is in Kampung Janda Baik, Bentong, Pahang — about an hour's drive from Kuala Lumpur, through the Bentong hills. Our address is No.3 Lorong Cheringin, Kampung Janda Baik, 28750 Bentong, Pahang.

We'll send detailed driving directions when your booking is confirmed. Open in Maps →

Yes. We can arrange private transfers from Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA), Sultan Abdul Aziz Shah Airport (Subang), or from anywhere in the Klang Valley. Just share your flight details or pickup address when you book.

Yes — private, on-site parking is available within the estate grounds for guests arriving by car. If you're bringing more than two vehicles, please let us know in advance so we can plan space accordingly.
