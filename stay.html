The Experience

Not just a room —
a rhythm of its own.

01

Private chef, on your clock

Meals are prepared in-house and served whenever you'd like — breakfast on the verandah, a long lunch by the pool, or dinner under the pavilion.

02

Estate-grown & locally sourced

Herbs and fruit from the garden, produce from farms within the valley. The kitchen works with what the land gives, season by season.

03

Slow mornings, long afternoons

No fixed schedule. Coffee on the deck, a walk through the grounds, an afternoon in the pool — the day is entirely yours.

Booking Information

Good to know

Check-in & check-out
Check-in from 3:00 PM. Check-out by 12:00 PM. Early check-in or late check-out available on request, subject to availability.
Minimum stay
Two nights minimum on weekends. Three nights on public holidays and peak seasons. Longer minimums may apply over festive periods.
Deposit & payment
A 50% deposit confirms your reservation. The balance is due 14 days before arrival. Payment by bank transfer, credit card, or FPX online banking.
Cancellation
Free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival. Within 14 days, the deposit is non-refundable. Cancellation terms confirmed at time of booking.
Breakfast & dining
Breakfast is included with every stay. Lunch and dinner can be arranged with our private chef — please allow 24 hours' notice for dietary requirements or special menus.
Children & families
Families are very welcome. Please let us know ages and any specific needs ahead of your stay so we can prepare accordingly.
Pets
We're currently not able to accommodate pets, to protect the estate's gardens and the surrounding wildlife.
Getting here
About an hour's drive from Kuala Lumpur, through the Bentong hills. Private airport transfers from KLIA can be arranged on request — just share your flight details.
