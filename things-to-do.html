On the Estate

Slow days,
close to home.

Everything at your pace, on your own grounds. The estate is designed for days that don't need to go anywhere.

01 — Morning

Sunrise yoga on the timber deck

An elevated deck facing the tree line — set with mats and a flask of hot tea, ready before you wake.

Daily · On request the evening before
02 — Afternoon

The pond, the pool, and long afternoons

Stocked with fresh tilapia, framed by tall grasses. Cast a line, or swim, or simply sit — the pond belongs to whoever is on the estate.

Anytime · Catch & release
03 — Evening

Private chef dinner under the pavilion

A slow table under timber and atap — local flavours, estate-grown herbs, and as many courses as you'd like.

On request · 24 hours' notice
A Day on the Estate

No fixed schedule —
but here's how it might go.

6 AM First light

Coffee on the verandah, then a slow walk through the gardens before the mist lifts off the hills.

9 AM Breakfast

Served at the dining pavilion — local fruit, eggs from the valley, coffee and tea, at whatever pace you like.

2 PM Outdoors

Fishing at the pond, a swim, or a drive to a nearby waterfall — back before the afternoon rain, if it comes.

7 PM Dinner

Chef-prepared dinner under the pavilion, then a slow evening on the deck. The estate goes quiet, and stays that way.

The estate runs on your time — not ours.

Around Janda Baik

The hills,
and beyond.

Waterfalls, hot springs, and quiet roads through the kampung hills — all within a short drive of the estate.

01

Waterfall & jungle trek

Guided half-day walk to a nearby waterfall — cool water, thick canopy, and a picnic on the rocks.

02

River bathing & picnics

Cool mountain water, minutes away.

03

Organic farm visits

Meet the growers of the valley.

04

Road cycling

Quiet kampung roads through the hills — bring your own bike or we'll help arrange a rental. Gentle morning rides with a stop for coffee in the village.
