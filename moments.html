Discover Moments

Across four acres, with timber pavilions, open gardens and a private pool — Singgah Sana is designed to host small, considered gatherings. A long lunch under the pavilion, a ceremony by the pond, a three-day leadership retreat. Entirely yours, for the duration of your stay.

The Gatherings Scroll to explore
01

Weddings

Ceremonies and receptions under timber pavilions and open gardens. The estate hosts up to 80 guests for a seated dinner, with the pool, pond and grounds as your backdrop.

Full estate buyout · 2-night minimum
02

Engagements

A quiet, private setting for the moment itself — just the two of you, or a small circle of family and close friends. We'll handle the candlelight, the setting and the silence.

Up to 20 guests · Half-day or overnight
03

Private Dinners

A slow table under the open-air pavilion, prepared by our chef — a long dinner for family, friends or a small circle of guests. Local produce, estate herbs, unhurried courses.

Up to 40 guests · Dinner only or overnight
04

Company Meetings & Conferences

A private, offline setting for leadership retreats, team offsites and end-of-year gatherings. Meeting space by day, the whole estate by night — no other guests, no interruptions.

Up to 30 delegates · Multi-day available
05

Private Workshops

Wellness retreats, craft workshops, writing retreats and small-group programmes — hosted across the estate's pavilions, gardens and quiet corners.

Up to 24 participants · Multi-day available
