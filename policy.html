01

Booking & Confirmation

Reservations at Singgah Sana may be made through our website, by email, or via WhatsApp. A reservation is considered confirmed only once the deposit has been received and a confirmation has been issued by our team.

What counts as a confirmed booking

  1. You submit an enquiry with your preferred dates, party size and pavilion.
  2. Our team confirms availability and issues a rate quote within the day.
  3. You pay the deposit (see section 02).
  4. We issue a written confirmation by email — at this point your reservation is locked in.

Booking for others

If you book on behalf of someone else, you are considered the responsible party for the reservation and are liable for the deposit, balance and any damages. Please ensure the primary guest is aware of the house rules in section 05.

"All rates quoted are valid for 7 days from the date of the quote — after that, our team will reconfirm availability and rates."

02

Rates & Payment

All rates are quoted in Malaysian Ringgit (RM) and are inclusive of applicable taxes and service charges, unless stated otherwise. Rates are per pavilion, per night, and are based on the standard occupancy of each pavilion.

What's included

  • Catered breakfast each morning
  • Daily housekeeping
  • Access to the pool, gardens, pond and shared areas
  • Filtered drinking water and estate-grown tea

Deposit & balance

Stage Amount & timing
Deposit 50% of the total — due at the time of confirmation
Balance Remaining 50% — due 14 days before arrival
Payment methods Credit / debit card, FPX online banking, or bank transfer

For stays during peak seasons or public holidays, different deposit and payment terms may apply. Our team will confirm these at the time of booking.

03

Cancellation & Changes

We understand that plans change. The following terms apply to all standard reservations — event and full-estate buyouts are subject to separate terms.

When you cancel What is refunded
14 days or more before arrival Full refund of deposit
7 to 13 days before arrival 50% of deposit refunded
Less than 7 days before arrival Deposit is non-refundable
No-show Full charge of the reservation applies

Date changes

You may request to move your reservation to different dates at no additional cost, subject to availability and at least 14 days' notice. Any rate difference for the new dates will be applied.

Cancellations by us

In the rare event that we must cancel your reservation — for example, due to an emergency or essential maintenance — we will refund your payment in full and help arrange alternative accommodation if possible.

"We strongly recommend travel insurance for all stays — it protects you against the unexpected."

05

House Rules

Singgah Sana is a private home, and the estate is shared with the local wildlife and community. These rules help keep it peaceful for everyone.

Quiet hours

Please keep noise to a minimum between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM. The estate sits within a small kampung and the surrounding hills carry sound.

Smoking

Smoking is not permitted inside any pavilion or enclosed area. If you smoke outdoors, please be mindful of other guests and use the ashtrays provided.

Guests & visitors

Only registered guests may stay overnight. Day visitors are welcome up to a limit — please inform our team in advance so we can plan accordingly.

Children & supervision

Children are very welcome but must be supervised at all times, especially around the pool, the pond, and the timber decks.

Pets

We're currently unable to accommodate pets — this protects the estate's gardens, the pond and the surrounding wildlife.

Wildlife & nature

Please do not disturb or feed the wildlife. The estate is home to birds, monitor lizards, tree frogs and other small animals. Lights along the tree line are kept off to protect them.

Candles & fire

Open flames (candles, incense, mosquito coils) may only be used in designated areas. Please do not use them inside timber pavilions or near the atap roofs.

"If something happens — a spill, a breakage, an accident — please tell us early. It's always easier to fix together than afterwards."

06

Damages & Liability

We understand accidents happen. Our aim is never to penalise — but we do ask that any damage or loss be reported to our team as soon as possible.

Accidental damage

Small accidents (a broken glass, a stained towel) are usually covered by our goodwill. Larger damage or missing items will be assessed and, where appropriate, charged at replacement cost.

Guest liability

Guests are responsible for their own belongings and for the behaviour of any visitors they invite to the estate. Please store valuables in the in-room safe provided.

Our liability

While we take every care to ensure a safe and comfortable stay, Singgah Sana cannot be held liable for loss or injury arising from circumstances beyond our reasonable control — including but not limited to weather events, wildlife, and the natural terrain of the estate.

07

Privacy & Data

We collect only the information we need to confirm and manage your stay — typically your name, email, phone number, country, dates of stay and any special requests.

How we use your data

  • To confirm and manage your reservation
  • To communicate with you before, during and after your stay
  • To comply with legal and accounting obligations

What we don't do

We never sell, rent or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your data is stored securely and accessed only by our small estate team.

Your rights

You may request a copy of the data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by writing to hello@singgah-sana.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

08

Events & Photography

Weddings, engagements, corporate retreats and private gatherings are hosted at Singgah Sana under a separate set of terms, agreed in writing before the event date.

Event bookings

Full-estate events require a larger deposit, a signed event agreement, and typically a longer minimum stay. Our events team will walk you through the specifics.

Photography & filming

Personal photography during your stay is always welcome. Commercial photography, brand shoots and film productions require prior written approval and may be subject to a location fee. Please write to us at hello@singgah-sana.com for details.

Privacy of other guests

If you're staying alongside other guests (which only happens on shared bookings), please be considerate when photographing the shared areas. Ask before including others in your shots.
