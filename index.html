The Estate

A private world, built in the spirit of the Malay kampung.

"Singgah Sana means a place to rest, to belong — that is the feeling we set out to build."

Tucked into the hills of Kampung Janda Baik, Singgah Sana is a five-bedroom private estate that reinterprets traditional Malay architecture for modern living. Timber posts, high pitched roofs and open verandahs echo the kampung houses of old Pahang, reimagined with the quiet comfort of a private resort.

Every material — from reclaimed chengal wood to hand-laid river stone — was chosen to age gracefully with the surrounding rainforest. A home that feels as though it has always belonged to the land.
